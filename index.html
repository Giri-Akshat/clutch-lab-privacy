Clutch Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. About Clutch Lab

Clutch Lab is a Discord bot that provides VALORANT player statistics, competitive match history, rank information, and estimated performance insights.

2. Information We May Store

Depending on the commands you use, Clutch Lab may store:

  • Your Discord user ID, to associate bot features with your account.
  • Your Riot ID (game name and tag) and selected region when you use player tracking.
  • Match statistics retrieved from supported third-party services.
  • Match records or rank information submitted through bot commands.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide player profiles, competitive match history, statistics, leaderboards, tracking, and estimated performance features.

4. Estimated Hidden MMR

Clutch Lab's Hidden MMR feature is an estimate based on available match data. It is not Riot Games' official hidden MMR and is not verified or endorsed by Riot Games.

5. Third-Party Services

Clutch Lab may use HenrikDev's unofficial VALORANT API to retrieve game data. Data availability and accuracy depend on that service and its terms and policies. Clutch Lab is not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games.

6. Storage and Security

Some information may be stored in local files to support bot functionality. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect stored information, but no method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to operate the bot. You may contact the bot owner to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with you.

8. Children's Privacy

Please do not submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information through the bot.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Clutch Lab changes. Updates will be published on this page.
